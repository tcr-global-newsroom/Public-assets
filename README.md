# TCR Global Newsroom: media kit

Official logos, avatars, banners and brand guidelines of **TCR Global Newsroom**, the independent news and data outlet covering TCR touring car racing.

- Website: [tcrglobalnewsroom.com](https://tcrglobalnewsroom.com)
- Accounts: **@tcrnewsroom** (FIA TCR World Tour) and **@tcrglobalnewsroom** (regional TCR series)
- Contact: [tcrglobalnewsroom@gmail.com](mailto:tcrglobalnewsroom@gmail.com)

## Brand usage guidelines

The logos, avatars, banners and other visuals in this repository are trademarks of TCR Global Newsroom. **All rights reserved.** No Creative Commons or open source licence applies to them.

### You may, without asking

Use the visuals **as they are** to illustrate or refer to TCR Global Newsroom: in an article, a video, a podcast, a presentation or a partner page. This includes commercial media.

### You may not

- **Modify the logos**: no change of colours or proportions, no cropping, no added effects or elements.
- **Imply a partnership or endorsement** by TCR Global Newsroom that does not exist.
- **Use them in your own logo or on a product**, whether it is sold or given away (merchandise, packaging, game content, etc.).

### Everything else

For any other use, ask first by email at [tcrglobalnewsroom@gmail.com](mailto:tcrglobalnewsroom@gmail.com).
